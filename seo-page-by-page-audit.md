# SEO Audit (abfallwirtschaft.vorstieg.eu)

Date: 2026-02-26
Scope: Visible on-page content + provided `<head>` for homepage. I did not have access to robots.txt, sitemap.xml, or per-page head metadata unless provided.

## Summary (Highest Impact)
- Homepage title/meta are generic and miss Austria + high-intent terms.
- Multiple typos across key pages (Begleitscheine, Abfallbilanz) harm quality signals.
- Mixed Du/Sie tone reduces professionalism and message clarity.
- Blog content is largely off-topic, diluting topical authority.
- Missing structured data (Organization, SoftwareApplication, FAQ).

## Homepage (`https://abfallwirtschaft.vorstieg.eu/`)
- Primary keyword focus: “ERP-Software Abfallwirtschaft” is present in H1.
- Issue: Hero copy is generic; does not mention Austria or high-intent tasks (Begleitschein, Abfallbilanz) early.
- Issue: Mixed tone (Du/Sie) reduces clarity and professionalism.
- Action: Rewrite H1/hero to include Austria + top tasks.
  - Example H1: “ERP für Abfallwirtschaft in Österreich: Begleitscheine & Abfallbilanz”
  - Example Sub: “Odoo-basiert. Für Entsorger, Recycler, Schrotthändler.”

## Begleitscheine (`https://abfallwirtschaft.vorstieg.eu/begleitscheine`)
- Strength: Strong topical focus on VEBSV / Begleitscheine. High-intent queries.
- Issue: Spelling errors (“Abfalle”, “mildepflichtigen”, “Abfallbillanz”).
- Action: Fix typos, add Austria context (“Österreich”, “EDM”) in first paragraph.
- Suggested title template: “VEBSV Begleitscheine in Odoo | Abfallwirtschaft Österreich”.

## Abfallbilanz (`https://abfallwirtschaft.vorstieg.eu/abfallbilanz`)
- Strength: Mentions EDM validation and Sankey diagrams; strong topical signals.
- Issue: Multiple spelling errors (“Aballbilanz”, “Belgeitscheine”, “ein mal”).
- Action: Fix typos; add “Österreich” and “EDM” in H1 or first paragraph.
- Suggested title template: “Abfallbilanz Österreich in Odoo | EDM-Validierung & Analyse”.

## Alle Funktionen (`https://abfallwirtschaft.vorstieg.eu/funktionen`)
- Strength: Rich feature list; good for long-tail keywords.
- Issue: Very broad, no prioritization or keyword grouping; one section appears truncated in HTML view.
- Action: Add H2 groupings by problem area: Compliance, Logistik, Handel, Produktion.
- Action: Insert internal links to Begleitscheine + Abfallbilanz with keyword-rich anchors.

## Entsorgungs-Unternehmen (`https://abfallwirtschaft.vorstieg.eu/entsorgungs-unternehmen`)
- Strength: Clear persona targeting; good for “Entsorgungsunternehmen Odoo” type queries.
- Issue: CTA generic; no Austria context; mixed Du/Sie tone.
- Action: Add Austria context and link to compliance pages.

## Recycling-Unternehmen (`https://abfallwirtschaft.vorstieg.eu/recycling-unternehmen`)
- Strength: Clear value proposition; strong operational keywords.
- Issue: Long blocks; no structured process content.
- Action: Add checklist section (Materialannahme, Analyse, Aufbereitung, Verkauf) with internal links.

## Schrotthändler (`https://abfallwirtschaft.vorstieg.eu/schrotthandler`)
- Strength: Strong niche relevance, mentions Waagenanbindung.
- Issue: Typo risk (e.g., “Wiegenoten”).
- Action: Fix typos; add Austria-specific compliance references if applicable.

## Blog (`https://abfallwirtschaft.vorstieg.eu/blog/it-im-unternehmen-3`)
- Major issue: Blog content mostly about Registrierkasse and generic Odoo topics, not Abfallwirtschaft. This dilutes topical authority for the core domain.
- Action options:
  - Split blog into Abfallwirtschaft category and move unrelated posts to another site/subdomain.
  - Add 3–5 Austria-specific waste-industry posts to rebalance topical signals.
